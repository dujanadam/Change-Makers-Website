About the Program

Ex Scintilla.
From a spark.

Change Makers is a workshop to teach kids how to use their ideas and skills to make the world better around them.

Our goal

Give kids the skills, space, and mentorship they need to start improving their community. And make them believe that they can do it.

This program is meant to give young people training and knowledge in how to deal with a problem like plastic pollution or poverty. And help them understand why, and form their identity into thinking: “I can have an impact on these problems.”

On the first day we tell them: you are going to do some hard stuff. Good attitude, and we'll help. And never say “I cannot do that,” because you can.

To change something is to combine three things:

🎯

Motivation

Autonomy, mastery and purpose. Without motivation and purpose we will never know why, and at the first sign of trouble we will give up. Our tank will run on empty very quickly.

🛠️

Knowledge

Training and ability. How to research a problem, plan, and actually do something about it. If you are driven, you will do what you can. We show you how.

💡

Self-Belief

Personal and Islamic. We are able to. But like the elephant, without self-belief we will never try.

Why here

Why Mecca.

Just like Muslims in the West are the face of Islam to non-Muslims, we are the face of Islam to Muslims. They come to Mecca and remember who we are. And we have a responsibility to uphold.

Every single Muslim's face turns towards Mecca. Every single Muslim tries at least once to visit Mecca. We have the chance to positively impact our entire ummah. This can be in hospitality, in how we take care of our city, or in our communities. But we should not take this lightly.

We need a strong core to have a strong body. So our responsibility is to take care of this core, and not be a weak part of this body.

We as humans are khulafaa on this earth, and we have a responsibility towards it. We work together to take care of the earth, and other people when they need it.

How it works

Seven steps.
One real project.

21–28 November, Saturday to Thursday, no Fridays. The girls do the work: choosing, planning, editing, presenting. We talk little, guide, and listen much.

1
What is a Change Maker?
Welcome and interactive introductions. What is a Change Maker, and how to be one. Examples: our own projects, Greta Thunberg, Malala, and others. The difference between awareness and impact. The challenges, and how to overcome them. The motto, and the elephant.
2
Problems & Choosing
Brainstorm problems, then choose one or two using a pros-and-cons list: ability, cost, interest. Each girl takes a specific job, like leader or documenter.
3
Research
Previous solutions, the cause of the problem, possible challenges, possible solutions, awareness vs. impact, ideas, and the goal.
4
Plan
Goal, jobs, when, how, where, why, who, cost and time.
5
Execute & Record
Do it: an event, a fundraiser, a drive. The work is recorded as it happens, to show as proof and to make a documentary. We film backs or covered faces, and faces only with permission.
6
Present
The girls practice, prepare and edit the recordings themselves. Then they present the recordings and a presentation to a real audience.
7
Reflect & Goodbye
How do you think we did, the good and the bad? What would you change? What are your goals? What problems do you still want to fix? Then a party, Change Makers t-shirts, and a certificate.

Who this is for

They need to want it.

Change Makers is for kids aged 9 to 14. This program is for girls only. No experience needed. We ask every applicant three things:

1. Are you willing to try new things?
2. Are you willing to work with a team?
3. Are you willing to get out of your comfort zone, even if it's hard and you might be afraid?

They will never do it if their reason is their parents said so.

You will change the world, each in your own area. Don't fight your natural interests. Instead, be the best in that area: football, video game design, medicine, law, the environment, girls' education. Don't know what you like yet? That's okay. Trying a real job on a real project is part of how you find out.

It's not up to us to change the world. It's up to Allah SWT. But we ask that He uses us in that process. We are on Allah's side, not He on ours.

Can and will change the world, Bi'ithnillah.
