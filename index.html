What is Change Makers?

The world has problems.
Your child has ideas.
We close the gap.

Change Makers is a hands-on summer program for kids aged 9–14. Over two weeks, participants identify a real problem in their community, research it, build a plan, and execute an actual project — finishing with a presentation to share what they've done and what they've learned.

This is not a passive classroom. Kids leave having done something real. Something they chose. Something they're proud of.

Why this program exists

Your child will shape tomorrow.
The question is whether they believe they can.

There's a reason so many people see a problem and say "someone should do something about that." It isn't apathy. It's three things: no sense of purpose, not knowing how, and most powerfully — not believing they can.

Kids learn this belief young. When we only give children information and never give them agency, they grow up watching problems get bigger and feeling smaller. But that belief doesn't go away when they turn 18.

"Baby elephants are tied to a stake with a rope they can't break. By the time they're strong enough to snap it easily, they've already stopped trying."

Change Makers is about cutting the rope early. About giving kids the experience of actually doing something — so that "I can make a difference" isn't just something they heard in a speech, it's something they've lived.

Your children will be architects of tomorrow whether they're ready for it or not. This program makes sure they're ready.
